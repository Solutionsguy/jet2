# Admin Analytics & Reporting Implementation Plan

This document tracks the progress of adding comprehensive reporting and analytics to the Aviator Admin Panel.

## 🟢 Status: Phase 3 (Leaderboards)

---

## 📊 1. Phase 1: Summary Cards (Today's Overview)
Quick snapshot of what's happening on the platform right now.
- [x] **New Users Today:** Count of registrations in the last 24h.
- [x] **Total Deposits Today:** Sum of successful deposits today.
- [x] **Total Withdrawals Today:** Sum of successful withdrawals today.
- [x] **Net Profit Today:** Deposits - Withdrawals.
- [x] **Active Bets Today:** Total volume of bets placed today.

## 📈 2. Phase 2: Visual Trend Graphs (7-Day Charts)
Visual representation of growth and cash flow using Chart.js.
- [x] **Cash Flow Chart:** Line graph showing Deposits vs Withdrawals for the last 7 days.
- [x] **User Growth Chart:** Bar chart showing new registrations per day.
- [ ] **Game Activity Chart:** Total bets placed per day. (Pending integration into chart)

## 🏆 3. Phase 3: Leaderboards (High Rollers)
Identify your most valuable users.
- [ ] **Top 10 Depositors:** Users who have funded the most.
- [ ] **Top 10 High Rollers:** Users with the highest total betting volume.
- [ ] **Most Winning Users:** Users with the highest profit from games.

## 📄 4. Phase 4: Advanced Reports & Filtering
Deep dive into historical data.
- [ ] **Date Range Filter:** Ability to see stats for "Last Month", "This Year", or custom dates.
- [ ] **Export to CSV/Excel:** Download reports for accounting.
- [ ] **P2P Efficiency Report:** Success rate and volume per peer number.
